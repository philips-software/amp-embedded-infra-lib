= BinaryPipe
:source-highlighter: highlight.js
:highlightjs-languages: protobuf

The BinaryPipe services provide arbitrary binary data transfer over an Echo link, addressed by a subchannel identifier. Availability is signalled by `BinaryPipeConnection`; data transfer is provided by `BinaryPipeSend` / `BinaryPipeReceive`, and by pattern-based variants used for testing and benchmarking.
