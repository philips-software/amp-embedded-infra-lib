== BLE Central GATT Session

Picks up after the *BLE Central Complete Flow* (or *BLE Central Bonded Reconnection*) has produced `CurrentState(connected)`. Widens the ATT bearer via MTU exchange, walks the Service / Characteristic / Descriptor discovery sequence, subscribes to notifications on the target characteristic, and tears the subscription down again. See xref:EchoServices/GattClient.adoc[GattClient] for per-method contracts.
